Session 1 — Do This First
Lower Body A
Squats · RDL · Hip Thrust · Calves · 55 min

5 min light walk or bike — easy pace
Hip circles × 10 each direction
Glute bridge × 15 bodyweight — wake up the glutes

Working Sets
1. Goblet Squat
Hold a dumbbell at your chest. Feet shoulder-width. Squat until thighs go below parallel — full depth. Chest up, heels on floor. This is your main squat movement — learn it well before adding weight.
Start: 8–10kg · Add 2kg when 3×12 feels comfortable with clean form
3 sets12 repsRest 90s
2. DB Romanian Deadlift
Push hips back — not down. Feel the stretch in the back of your legs. Keep your back flat. Drive hips forward to stand. Feel the hamstrings working, not lifting heavy.
Start: 8–10kg each hand
3 sets12 repsRest 90s
3. DB Hip Thrust
Upper back on bench. Dumbbell on hips. Drive hips up. Squeeze glutes hard at the top — hold for 2 full seconds. Lower slowly. One of the best exercises you can do for glute development.
Start: 10–14kg · The 2-second hold is not optional
3 sets15 repsRest 75s
4. Reverse Lunge
Step backward — not forward. Control the descent. Front knee over ankle. Drive through front heel to return. Alternate legs each rep.
Start: bodyweight · Add 4–6kg dumbbells when BW feels easy
3 sets10/legRest 75s

A1. Leg Extension
Full lockout at top — hold 1 second. Lower slowly over 3 counts. Feel it in the front of your thigh, not your knee.

A2. Seated Calf Raise
Lower all the way down, pause 2 seconds. Rise to full height. Don't rush — calves respond to full range and volume.

Session 2 — Do This Second
Upper Body
Chest · Back · Shoulders · Arms · 55 min
▾
Warm-Up — 6 min
Arm circles × 15 each direction
Shoulder rolls × 15 forward and backward
Light dumbbell press × 10 — groove the pattern
Dead hang × 20s — open the lats
Working Sets
1. DB Chest Press
Flat or incline bench — whichever is available. Lower until you feel a stretch across your chest. Press to full lockout. Don't arch your lower back.
Start: 6–8kg each hand · Add 1–2kg when 3×12 feels easy
3 sets12 repsRest 75s
2. Lat Pulldown
Pull bar to upper chest. At the top, let arms fully extend and feel the lats stretch. Squeeze at the bottom for 1 second. Builds the width of your back.
Start: 20–30kg · Progress when 12 reps feel easy
3 sets12 repsRest 75s
3. Seated DB Shoulder Press
Sit upright — no leaning back. Lower until elbows drop below shoulder level. Press straight up to full lockout. Keep it strict. Builds shoulder roundness and width.
Start: 4–6kg each · Small increments — shoulders are a smaller muscle group
3 sets12 repsRest 75s
4. Seated Cable Row
Sit tall — no rocking. Pull handle to lower chest. Squeeze shoulder blades together, hold 1 second. Return slowly over 3 counts. Builds mid-back thickness.
Start: 15–25kg · Feel your back working, not just your arms
3 sets12 repsRest 75s
Superset ↓ — go straight from A1 to A2, rest 60s after both
A1. DB Lateral Raise
Slight bend in elbows. Raise to shoulder height, lead with elbows. Lower slowly over 3 counts. Creates visible shoulder width. Keep weight light enough to control fully.
Start: 3–5kg · Technique over weight
3 sets15 repsRest —
A2. DB Bicep Curl
Elbows fixed at sides. Rotate palm upward at the top. Alternate arms. Control the lowering — don't let the weight drop.
Start: 5–8kg each
3 sets10/armRest 60s

Stand facing away from cable. Push hips back like a Romanian deadlift. Feel the stretch. Drive hips forward. Pure glute and hamstring — keep back flat and let the hips do the work.
Light-moderate cable weight

The One Rule That Changes Everything
You mentioned you skip meals sometimes. At 53kg with a goal of 60kg, that's the main thing holding you back. You can't build 7kg of muscle on an inconsistent food intake. Before anything else — commit to three meals every day. Start there. Everything else follows.
🌅
Morning — Before Gym
Breakfast
~500 kcal
Option A — 5 minutes
3 boiled or scrambled eggs + 2 slices bread + 1 banana
Protein and carbs before training. Easy to make. Eat 60–90 min before your session.
Option B — No cooking
Greek yogurt 200g + granola or mixed nuts + 1 fruit
Open the fridge, eat, leave. There is no reason to skip breakfast when this option exists.
Option C — Most filling
Oats 80g cooked in milk + 1 banana + honey
Slow release energy — you'll stay full until lunch and have fuel for the entire session.
☀️
Midday
Lunch — Your Biggest Meal
~700 kcal
The Framework
Protein + starch + vegetables — apply to whatever is available
Protein: chicken, fish, beef, eggs, or beans. Starch: rice, potato, pasta, or bread. Vegetables: whatever is there. You don't need to cook anything special — just apply the framework to what's already available.
The Drink Rule
Water only with meals — no juice, no soda
Sugary drinks add hundreds of empty calories with no benefit for muscle building.
🍫
Afternoon
Snack — Don't Skip This
~300 kcal
Best Options
Groundnuts + banana · 2 boiled eggs + fruit · Bread + peanut butter · Greek yogurt
The afternoon gap between lunch and dinner is where ectomorphs lose their calories for the day. This snack fills that gap with something that has both protein and energy.
🌙
Evening
Dinner — Feeds Overnight Recovery
~600 kcal
Same Framework as Lunch
Protein + starch + vegetables · Slightly smaller than lunch
Most of your muscle repair happens while you sleep. Dinner is what fuels that recovery. Don't skip it because you're tired.
If the Scale Isn't Moving
Weight not increasing after 2 weeks — add one more snack or increase lunch portion. Your body needs more fuel. Fix the food before changing the training.
4 Rules to Keep
01
Show Up 4 Times Per Week — Any 4 Mornings
No fixed days needed. Pick any 4 mornings each week. Do sessions in order 1, 2, 3, 4. Rest at least one day between sessions where you can.
02
Eat 3 Meals Every Day
Not a perfect diet — just three meals. Breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Even small meals count. This single habit is what moves the scale from 53 to 60kg.
03
Add Weight When Sessions Feel Easy
When 3×12 feels manageable with good form, add 1–2kg. Log your weights in the Progress tab. If weights aren't going up over time, your body has no reason to grow.
04
Weigh Yourself Every Sunday Morning
After bathroom, before food. Same conditions every week. Target: +0.3kg per week. Not moving for 2 weeks means eat more.
