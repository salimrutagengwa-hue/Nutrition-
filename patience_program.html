Patience — I built this program around everything you shared with me. Your schedule, your gym, your goals, your starting point. This is not a generic plan. How Your Program Works
01
4 sessions per week — any 4 mornings. You mentioned your schedule isn't fixed and that's fine. Pick any 4 mornings that work for you each week. Do the sessions in order: Session 1, then 2, then 3, then 4. Rest at least one day between sessions where you can.
02
Follow each session exactly as written. Every exercise is here for a reason. Don't skip movements because they look unfamiliar — the cues explain everything you need to know.
03
Add weight when sessions feel easy. When 3 sets of 12 reps feel manageable with good form, add 1–2kg next time. Log your weights in the Progress tab so you can track every session.
04
Eat 3 meals every day. Your nutrition plan is in the Nutrition tab. This is as important as the training — more important, actually. You cannot build what you don't feed.

The One Rule That Changes Everything
You mentioned you skip meals sometimes. At 53kg with a goal of 60kg, that's the main thing holding you back. You can't build 7kg of muscle on an inconsistent food intake. Before you think about macros or protein counts — just commit to three meals every day. Start there. Everything else follows from that.
🌅
Morning — Before Gym
Breakfast
~450–500 kcal
Option A — 5 minutes
3 boiled or scrambled eggs + 2 slices bread + 1 banana
Protein and carbs together before training. Easy to make. Eat 60–90 minutes before your gym session.
Option B — No cooking needed
Greek yogurt 200g + granola or mixed nuts + 1 piece of fruit
Open the fridge, eat, leave. There is no reason to skip breakfast when this option exists.
Option C — Most filling
Oats 80g cooked in milk + 1 banana + a drizzle of honey
Slow release energy — you'll stay full until lunch and have fuel for the entire training session.
☀️
Midday
Lunch — Your Biggest Meal
~650–700 kcal
The Framework
Protein + starch + vegetables — apply this to whatever food is available
Protein: chicken, fish, beef, eggs, or beans. Starch: rice, potato, pasta, or bread. Vegetables: whatever's there. This combination covers everything your body needs to grow. You don't need to cook anything special — just apply the framework to what's already available.
The Drink Rule
Water only with meals — no juice, no soda
Sugary drinks with meals add several hundred empty calories with no nutritional benefit for muscle building. Water with every meal is a simple switch that adds up significantly over time.
🍫
Afternoon
Snack — Don't Skip This
~250–300 kcal
Best Options
Groundnuts + banana · 2 boiled eggs + fruit · Bread + peanut butter · Greek yogurt
The afternoon gap between lunch and dinner is where ectomorphs lose their calories for the day. This snack fills that gap with something that has both protein and energy. It's also what stops you from arriving at dinner too hungry and eating poorly.
🌙
Evening
Dinner — Feeds Overnight Recovery
~550–600 kcal
Same Framework as Lunch
Protein + starch + vegetables · Slightly smaller portion than lunch
Most of your muscle repair happens while you sleep — not while you train. Dinner is what fuels that recovery. Don't skip it because you're tired. Apply the same framework as lunch and you're done.
If the Scale Isn't Moving
If your weight hasn't increased by 0.3kg per week for 2 consecutive weeks — add one more snack to your day or increase your lunch portion. Your body is telling you it needs more fuel. Fix the food before changing anything in the training.
4 Rules to Keep
01
Show Up 4 Times Per Week — Any 4 Mornings
You have no fixed schedule and that's completely fine. Pick any 4 mornings each week. Do the sessions in order — 1, 2, 3, 4. Rest at least one day between sessions where you can. That's the only scheduling rule.
02
Eat 3 Meals Every Day
Not a perfect diet — just three meals. Breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Even small meals count. This single habit, done consistently, is what moves the scale from 53 to 60kg.
03
Add Weight When Sessions Feel Easy
When 3 sets of 12 reps feel manageable with good form, add 1–2kg next session. Log your weights in the Progress tab. If your weights aren't going up over time, your body has no reason to grow.
04
Weigh Yourself Every Sunday Morning
After bathroom, before food — same conditions every week. Target is +0.3kg per week. Not moving for 2 weeks means eat more. Moving faster than 0.5kg per week means reduce the afternoon snack slightly.
